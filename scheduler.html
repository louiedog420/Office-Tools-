How to use this page
  • Add each person, then type a start, an end, and where they are going.
  • Hours are start time to end time. If lunch is unpaid and not already left out of the times, type the lunch minutes. Lunch is taken off a shift only when that shift is 5 hours or longer.
  • A warning appears if someone is scheduled over 40 hours, if two of their shifts overlap, if they are scheduled on a day with approved time off, or if a day has fewer people than you said you need.
  • Approved time off comes from the Time off page, matched by name.
  • Use Copy last week when the new week looks like the old one.
  • Print when you want a copy for the wall. Slide the grid sideways on a phone.
