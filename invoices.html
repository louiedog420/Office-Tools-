How to use this page
  • Bill to pay is money you owe a vendor. Invoice you sent is money a customer owes you.
  • Unpaid items turn red when the due date has passed. Items due in the next 14 days are called out at the top.
  • Mark something paid when the money has moved. Paid items stay on the list so you can look back.
  • The invoice form at the bottom is for printing. You can also save it onto the list.
