How the colors work
  • Red means a date has already passed.
  • Orange means something is due within 30 days, including today.
  • Gold means something is due in 31 to 60 days.
  • The list is sorted by the soonest of registration, insurance, and inspection. The most urgent unit is first.
  • The VIN is the long ID on the vehicle. It is not a Social Security number. Do not type SSNs or bank numbers here.
