How to use this page
  • A request is vacation, sick time, or another day off. Set it to Pending, Approved, or Denied.
  • A call-out is someone who did not come in. A tardy is someone who was late.
  • Day counts are calendar days, including the first and last day. They are not only work days.
  • Names you already saved in Payroll or the Schedule show up as suggestions.
  • The schedule warns you when someone is booked on a day that is approved time off. The names need to match.
