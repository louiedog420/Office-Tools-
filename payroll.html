How overtime is figured (short and plain)

In Maryland, overtime generally follows the federal rule: hours over 40 in one workweek are overtime. Overtime is usually an extra half of the person’s blended hourly rate, on top of straight-time pay for every hour worked.

  • A biweekly paycheck is two separate weeks. Someone who works 50 hours one week and 30 the next has 10 overtime hours, not zero. The extra hours are not averaged over 80.
  • Type regular hours like 8 or 8.5. Or type clock times like 8:30a / 5p or 8:30a-5p. Lunch is taken out only when you use clock times.
  • Snow time: hours worked between 4:00 PM and 7:00 PM (you can change that window) get an extra $5.00 an hour on top of the regular rate. Those hours still count toward the 40-hour overtime week. If you type clock times, snow hours are figured automatically. If you type a number like 8, type the snow hours in the small “Snow” box under that day (or put the whole week’s snow hours under one day).
  • When someone has overtime AND snow pay, federal rules say the extra $5 has to be folded into the overtime rate. Example: 45 hours at $20, with 5 snow hours. Straight-time 45 × $20 = $900.00. Snow extra 5 × $5 = $25.00. Blended rate ($900 + $25) ÷ 45 = about $20.56. Overtime extra: 5 × half of that = $51.39. Gross pay = $900.00 + $25.00 + $51.39 = $976.39.
  • This calculator finds gross pay only — the amount before taxes. Your payroll company or accountant still handles tax withholding.
  • Do not type Social Security numbers or bank account numbers in any box. This page does not need them.
