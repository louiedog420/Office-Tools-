B and B: Government Contract Forms

This page lists every form B and B may need after winning a government contract. Each one says what it's for, who fills it out, when it's due and where to find the official form. Set a status on each one as you go. Your choices are saved on this device.

Check with the contracting officer first.This is a general list. The exact forms depend on who the contract is with (federal, Maryland or county) and the clauses written in it. Ask the contracting officer for the full list of required documents, and check the contract's clause list.

Start here

  1. Read the award letterFind who the contract is with, the contracting officer's name and email, and the type of work (construction or services).
  2. Check registrationsFederal contracts need SAM.gov showing Active. Maryland contracts need eMMA and good standing with SDAT.
  3. Sign and sendSign the award or contract page, the Maryland affidavits if it's a state job, and a W-9.
  4. Insurance and bondsAsk your insurance agent for the certificate of insurance. On construction, ask your surety for the bonds.
  5. Set pay ratesLook up the wage determination and set payroll to match. Hang the required posters.
  6. Hiring paperworkDo the I-9, W-4 and MW507 for each worker, plus E-Verify if the contract requires it.
  7. TrucksCheck the USDOT number, driver medical cards and truck registrations.
  8. Weekly and monthlyConstruction jobs need certified payroll every week. Set up invoicing in the system the contract names.
