Do not collect private numbers here

Never type a Social Security number or a bank account number in any box. The checklist only records that the paper form is in the file. Give Form I-9, the W-4, Maryland MW 507, and the direct deposit form to your payroll company. This page is not a safe place for those numbers.

How to use this page
  • Add an applicant and move the stage as you go: New application, Interview, Offer, Hired, or Not moving forward.
  • When you mark someone Hired, a new-hire checklist is added if they do not already have one.
  • Check a box when that paper is done. Emergency contact can be a name and phone only.
